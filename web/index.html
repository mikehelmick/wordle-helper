How to use it
  1. Type the five-letter word you guessed.
  2. Click each tile (or press Space) to cycle its colour to match the game.
  3. Press Enter, or use Record turn, to record it.

On the on-screen keyboard, tap Wrong spot or Correct first and the whole keyboard changes colour: the next letter you tap is entered in that colour, then it reverts. Tap the same colour again to cancel it.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type leaves this page, and the page makes no network requests at all.
